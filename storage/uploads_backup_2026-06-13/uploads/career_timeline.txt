Career Timeline for Dr. Supreet Khare: After completing his medical education in India, he worked at Fortis Memorial Research Institute, Medanta - The Medicity, and Paras Hospital. He then moved to the USA to complete his residency at the University of Arizona, where he served as Chief Resident at the South Campus. Currently, he is the Managing Director, CEO, and Internal Medicine Consultant at Health First Multi Specialty Clinic in Dwarka, New Delhi.
