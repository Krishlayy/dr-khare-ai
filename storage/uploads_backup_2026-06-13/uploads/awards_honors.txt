Dr. Supreet Khare has received numerous awards including the prestigious Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) Scholarship consecutively for 5 years (2010-2014). He also won the Champion's Trophy awarded by Nobel Laureate Dr. Robin Warren. Other awards include Best Paper Awards, Gold Medals in Pathology and Microbiology during MBBS, the McGraw Hill Award 2017, and the Brig. S.K. Mazumdar Young Scientist Award 2013.
