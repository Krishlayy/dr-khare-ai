DR. SUPREET KHARE — RESEARCH, PUBLICATIONS & ACADEMIC CONTRIBUTIONS

OVERVIEW

Dr. Supreet Khare is a physician-scientist, researcher, and academic contributor whose work spans clinical medicine, bioinformatics, genomics, proteomics, pharmacovigilance, medical education, and public health research. Throughout his academic and professional career, he has actively participated in research projects, scientific publications, conference presentations, and international academic collaborations.
