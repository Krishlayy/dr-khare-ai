DR. SUPREET KHARE — PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

OVERVIEW

Dr. Supreet Khare is a board-certified Internal Medicine physician, medical researcher, healthcare entrepreneur, and executive leader with extensive international clinical, academic, and research experience. He currently serves as Managing Director and CEO of Compendious Med Works Pvt. Ltd. and has held leadership, clinical, research, and academic positions in the United States and India.

Dr. Khare is recognized for his achievements in medical research, innovation, healthcare leadership, occupational medicine, neurology, internal medicine, and medical education.

SHORT BIO

Dr. Supreet Khare is a physician, researcher, entrepreneur, and healthcare executive with training in Internal Medicine and Occupational Medicine. His career spans clinical medicine, medical research, healthcare leadership, education, public health, and innovation. He has received numerous national and international awards, published scientific research across multiple disciplines, and held leadership roles in healthcare organizations in the United States and India.
