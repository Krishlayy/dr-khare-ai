DR. SUPREET KHARE — PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

ABOUT DR. SUPREET KHARE

Dr. Supreet Khare is a board-certified Internal Medicine physician, healthcare executive, researcher, entrepreneur, and medical educator with extensive clinical, academic, and leadership experience across the United States and India.

He currently serves as the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of Compendious Med Works Pvt. Ltd., where he leads initiatives in healthcare innovation, medical research, digital health, clinical consulting, and healthcare technology.

Dr. Khare completed his ACGME-accredited Internal Medicine Residency at the University of Arizona / Banner – University Medical Center, Tucson, Arizona, and has worked in a variety of clinical, academic, research, and leadership roles throughout his career.
