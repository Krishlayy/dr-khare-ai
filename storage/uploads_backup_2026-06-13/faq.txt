# FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ) ABOUT DR. SUPREET KHARE

Q: Who is Dr. Supreet Khare?
A: Dr. Supreet Khare is a board-certified physician, medical researcher, entrepreneur, and healthcare executive. He completed his Internal Medicine residency at the University of Arizona and is currently the Managing Director and CEO of Compendious Med Works Pvt. Ltd. in Pune, India.

Q: Where did Dr. Khare go to medical school?
A: Dr. Khare attended the Armed Forces Medical College (AFMC) in Pune, India from 2009 to 2015 for his MBBS degree.

Q: Where did Dr. Khare complete his residency?
A: Dr. Khare completed his Internal Medicine Residency at the University of Arizona / Banner University Medical Center (2017-2020) and his Occupational Medicine Residency at the University of Pennsylvania (2020-2021).

Q: Where did Dr. Khare work before moving to the USA?
A: Before moving to the USA, Dr. Khare worked as a Junior Resident in the Department of Emergency Medicine at Deen Dayal Upadhyay Hospital in New Delhi, India from Jan 2016 to Jun 2016.

Q: Did Dr. Khare have a role at Lompoc Valley Medical Center?
A: Yes, Dr. Khare served as a Member of the Board of Directors at Lompoc Valley Medical Center.

Q: Did Dr. Khare have a leadership role at California Medical Behavioral Health?
A: Yes, Dr. Khare served as an Executive Board Member at California Medical Behavioral Health.

Q: What languages does Dr. Khare speak?
A: He speaks English, Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Marathi, and has basic proficiency in Spanish.

Q: Has Dr. Khare published any fiction novels?
A: Yes, he is the author of the fiction novel "Tales of Enkanto - A Paradoxical Beginning" published in 2012.

Q: Where is Compendious Med Works located?
A: Compendious Med Works Pvt. Ltd. is located in Pune, India.

Q: Where is Health First Multi Specialty Clinic located?
A: Health First Multi Specialty Clinic is located in Pune, India.

Q: What professional certificates does Dr. Khare hold?
A: Dr. Khare holds an ECFMG Certificate.

Q: Is Dr. Khare registered with any medical councils in India?
A: Yes, he is registered with the Delhi Medical Council and the Rajasthan Medical Council.

Q: Has Dr. Khare received any academic gold medals?
A: Yes, Dr. Khare has received academic gold medals during his medical training.
