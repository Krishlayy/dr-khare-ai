# RESEARCH & PUBLICATIONS: Dr. Supreet Khare

## Overview
Dr. Supreet Khare is a physician-scientist with expertise in clinical medicine, bioinformatics, genomics, proteomics, pharmacovigilance, and public health research.

## Key Research Experience
- Institute of Bioinformatics (IOB), Bangalore, India (Affiliated with Johns Hopkins University, USA): Worked as a Research Fellow involving proteomics, genomics, computational biology, and mass spectrometry.
- University of Arizona / Banner University Medical Center: Conducted quality improvement research on Diabetic Ketoacidosis in the ICU.
- COPE Trial: Served as a Principal Investigator for the COPE Trial at the University of Arizona.

## Notable Publications & Topics
- "Pseudodementia": Peer-reviewed journal article by Khare S, Khare S, Seth D.
- "The Role of Academic Research and Teaching in Addressing Health Inequities" (2015).
- Published research spanning Neurology, Parkinson's Disease, Acute Leukemia Immunophenotyping, Chronic Widespread Pain, and Lactic Acidosis Following Surgery.
- Published in international journals like Cureus, focusing on Chest Infections, COVID-19 pulmonary complications, and steroids.

## Awards & Presentations
- ICMR Research Scholarship: First and only undergraduate medical student in India to receive the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) Scholarship consecutively for 5 years (2010-2014).
- Karmic Conference (2013): Winner of the Champion's Trophy, awarded by Nobel Laureate Dr. Robin Warren.
- Regional Research Conference (Belgaum, 2011): Winner of Best Paper Award.
- MEDICON Conference (2012): Winner of Best Paper Award.
- International Medical Summer School Conference (Manchester, UK, 2013): Youngest AFMC cadet selected and first Indian student to present research internationally on a full scholarship.
- Nominated for a Young Scientist award.
