Elsevier, New Delhi, India 
Elsevier Student Ambassador (ESA) 2014, 
 
Elsevier India is a reputed publishing company which selected around 100 medical students 
all over India via step by step process to be selected as Elsevier Student Ambassador (ESA) 
2014. We were given several voluntary tasks including editing medical literature, 
contributing to online MyPGMEE Qbanks, writing book reviews and contributing to

10/2013 - Present 
NIMHANS - Positive Psychology Unit, Bangalore, India 
Student Volunteer, Dr. Seema Mehrotra 
 
I have worked enthusiastically with the Positive Psychology unit (PPU) of NIMHANS 
(National Institute of Mental Health and Neurological Sciences). PPU was established in 
1954 as the Department of Psychology and Human Relations in the then All India Institute of 
Mental Health. Since then PPU aims to provide comprehensive and evidence-based 
psychological services to patients and their caregivers. PPU conducts programs that focus on 
reaching out to the public and various target groups in the community to enhance 
public awareness and empower communities to deal with challenges to mental health and to 
maintain or enhance their well- being. I have zealously participated in any such program 
conducted by NIMHANS-Positive Psychology Unit.

IFMSA-MSAI, Lucknow, India 
State President,

publishes research papers free of cost from the medical students.

Deep Griha Society, Pune, India 
Volunteer Worker, 
 
Deep Griha Society is an independent charitable organisation working to better the lives of 
people living in the slums of Pune, India. Since 1975, Deep Griha has constantly expanded 
and adapted its services to empower the disadvantaged community through support and 
education, giving people the skills and confidence they need to improve their lives. I worked 
as one of the field workers whenever I could get the opportunity and conducted small lectures 
to educate people on various health issues.
