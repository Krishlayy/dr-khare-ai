Role of High Dose Omeprazole (HDO) on Salivary Amylase and Bicarbonate Secretions. 
 
It remains to be established whether omeprazole has a stimulatory effect of its own on 
bicarbonate and sodium and potassium secretion, which might contribute to the therapeutic 
gain or it reduces the secretions producing adverse effects. Thus it becomes important to 
evaluate the role of High Dose Omeprazole on Sodium, Potassium and Bicarbonate 
secretions. Thirty healthy volunteers were administered a 60 mg dose of Omeprazole and 
saliva was collected before and after the drug administration twice daily for a week. All the 
saliva samples were analysed for sodium, potassium and bicarbonate. My study showed how 
high dose Omeprazole effects the secretions in the stomach and its protective property in 
curing gastric ulcers and GERD but still the mechanism of action remains speculative and 
further studies should be carried out for the same.

AFMC Departmental Project (Surgery), India 
Student Researcher (Departmental Project), Air Cmd A K Pujahari, Prof and Head Surgery 
 
A Case report on a strange presentation of Emphysematous Cholecystitis. 
 
Emphysematous cholecystitis is an acute infection of the gallbladder wall caused by gas-
forming organisms. It is infrequent with insidious onset and diagnosed by the use of 
radiographs detecting presence of air within the gallbladder wall or lumen. The report 
describes the case of a 42 year old alcoholic male who presented with sudden onset of right 
upper quadrant of abdomen pain, fever and bilious vomiting of two days duration. The patient 
did not have symptoms of jaundice. Emergency partial cholecystectomy was done and the 
culture directed antibiotics were given. The patient was followed up and he remained 
asymptomatic. We concluded that emergency laparoscopic partial cholecystectomy is an 
alternate to open surgery in this high risk pathology.

Assessment of health seeking behaviour and needs of adolescents: A cross sectional 
study. 
 
The current health care services are not oriented towards adolescent health care and needs 
assessment regarding health behaviour. The study was done to assess knowledge, attitude and 
health seeking behaviour among adolescents. A questionnaire having 64 items assessing 
different aspects of Adolescents health fulfilling above mentioned objectives was used to 
assess 280 adolescents from schools. Following were used for assessment: Safety, addictions, 
knowledge of sex, views about themselves, habits, views about school, healthcare views 
and family views. The adolescents were aware of their safety, 90% of them did not have any 
addictions. Very few adolescents had knowledge of sex. Knowledge regarding health care 
facilities was poor. The health seeking behaviour and needs of Adolescent health are unmet 
for which appropriate interventions should be taken.

Incidence of unexpected Post -Operative Lactic Acidosis in cases of prolonged surgeries. 
 
The study was undertaken to find the incidence of lactic acidosis and its effects on outcome 
of the patient and to find correlation between hyperlactatemia and intra-operative 
management and make suggestions based on the study. All patients undergoing surgery for 
more than 3 hours were included. These nine parameters were considered for the study - pH, 
pO2, pCO2, K+, Na+, HCO3-, lactate. Among all the nine parameters, lactate levels were the 
most significant value in determining incidence of postoperative lactic acidosis. The study 
has demonstrated that elevated blood lactate level is associated with adverse outcome, and 
monitoring the blood lactate level during and after surgery is a valuable tool in identifying the 
patients which have the potential to deteriorate.

Student Researcher (MUHS), Grp Captain Salil Gupta, Prof & Senior Neurologist 
 
Neuropsychiatric Non Motor symptoms and their effect on Quality of Life in patients of 
Parkinsonism disease. 
 
The non-motor symptoms (NMS) of Parkinson's disease continue to be poorly recognized and 
inadequately treated in contrast with motor symptoms and a modern holistic approach to 
treatment of Parkinson's disease should therefore include recognition and assessment of non-
motor symptoms. They were asked to fill the following scales: MDAS, DASS, AES-1 and 
PQOL. This study showed the variation of non-motor symptoms in Parkinson patients. Lack 
of motivation, depression, anxiety, stress, delirium is common in Parkinson patients which 
affects the Quality of life of PD patients. Delayed detection of NMS may lead to disability 
and poor quality of life, increasing the cost of care of PD in the society. We concluded that 
Recognition of non-motor symptoms is therefore essential for the holistic management of PD 
and the importance of a multidisciplinary approach.

Department of Physiology, AFMC, India 
Student researcher (ICMR), Col Anuj Chawla, Prof and Head Physiology 
 
Effect of Change In Body Position On Resting Electrocardiogram In Young Healthy 
Adults. 
 
The study was done to investigate the effect of a change in body position on the frontal plane 
ECG. The ECG was recorded in four body positions, supine, reclining with the trunk flexed 
at 70o, sitting upright and standing in thirty healthy subjects. The mean frontal plane QRS 
axis recorded in the supine and standing positions was comparable. The reclining and sitting 
ECG show significant variation of the mean QRS axis as compared to the supine position. 
The T wave axis was found to be comparable in the supine and standing positions. I came up 
with significant conclusions at the end of six months. The corrected QT interval showed a 
significant change with a change in body position from supine to standing. All the subjects 
showed similar ECG changes but differences in the magnitude of the changes with change in 
body position.
